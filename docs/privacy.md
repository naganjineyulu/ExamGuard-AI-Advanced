# Privacy & Review Principles

ExamGuard AI is designed as an assistive proctoring system.

- Obtain explicit consent before camera-based monitoring.
- Explain what signals are collected.
- Use configurable retention.
- Prefer on-device processing for webcam analysis.
- Treat risk scores as indicators, not proof.
- Require human review for consequential decisions.
- Provide an audit trail for reviewers.
- Do not record unrelated browsing activity.
